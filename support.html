Start here. Enthousia does not talk to a server, so most problems are not connection problems. If something looks wrong, the useful thing to tell me is what is on the screen and what you expected instead — a screenshot is enough. Email: hangzhou.gxm@gmail.com

I bought the full reading and it still says locked

Open Settings (the gear tab) and tap Restore purchase. The purchase lives on your Apple Account, not with us, so this works on any iPhone signed into the same Apple Account — including a new phone.

If Restore says nothing is on the account, the charge probably went to a different Apple Account, or it failed and Apple refunded it automatically. Check Settings → your name → Media & Purchases → Purchase History. If the line is there and Enthousia still will not unlock, email me the date and the last four digits of the order number and I will find out what the app is doing wrong.

The unlock button shows no price

That means the app could not get a price from the App Store on this launch, and it would rather show you nothing than invent a number. It is usually airplane mode, a very old iOS, or Apple's store being briefly unreachable. Reopen the app once you have signal; if it keeps happening, tell me and include your iOS version.

Does it work on a plane / with no signal?

Yes. Everything you need is inside the app: the eighteen figures, their stories, and the text of every reading. The only moment that needs the network is the purchase itself, because Apple takes the payment.

Where do my questions go?

Nowhere. They are written to one file inside the app's own storage on your iPhone, encrypted while the phone is locked. The app has no networking code, so there is no route out — this is not a policy promise, it is how the binary is built. Full detail: privacy policy.

How do I delete a reading?

Swipe left on it in Archive and tap Delete. It is gone from the device immediately, and there is no copy anywhere else to ask about. Deleting the app removes the rest.

Is this an AI chatbot?

No. There is no model in the app and no service behind it. The text was written by hand and packaged with the app; the app selects from it based on your question and the three figures you draw. The same question and the same three figures always give you the same document — which is the opposite of what a chatbot does.

Why did I get the same three figures twice?

The draw is random per question, and eighteen figures taken three at a time is not as many combinations as it feels. Repeated hands are a property of the deck, not a bug and not a sign.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles every payment and every refund for this app; we never see your card and cannot issue one yourself. Contact Apple via reportaproblem.apple.com and they will tell you yes or no.

Should I take what it says seriously?

Take it as a way to notice what you already think. Enthousia writes about figures from myth, and myth is useful precisely because it is not about you specifically — it gives you distance. It is not medical, psychological, financial or legal advice, and it does not predict anything. If you are in a real crisis, this is the wrong app to be asking.
